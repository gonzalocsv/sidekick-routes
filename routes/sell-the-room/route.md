## Lens
You were asked in as the advisor, so the answer comes first. A question without a stated hypothesis is routine, not
Inquiry 4+. A recommendation that names what it costs and what would change it is Influence 4+; the room reusing
your term or number later is Influence 5. Ending on "thoughts?" or "let's take it offline" is Closing 1; asking who
decides and by when is Closing 4+.

## Focus tracks
- Lead with a view: states a recommendation before the third question (Influence)
- Name the trade-off: every recommendation says what it costs and what would change it (Clarity)
- Ask for the decision: asks for a decision, or an owner and a date, never "thoughts?" (Closing)

## Drills
- Client CMO: "Three agencies have told us the same thing this month. Why you?" (Influence)
- Their CFO, joining late: "What is the payback period on this pilot?" (Inquiry)
- Steering group: "Can we park the decision until next quarter?" (Closing)
- Head of product: "We've tried AI pilots before. They never left the lab." (Influence)
- Client lead: "Walk me through the options again?" (Clarity)
- Their CEO: "So what would you do in my place?" (Influence)
