## Lens
In an interview the candidate's story is the surface; the example is the evidence. Inquiry 4+ means the same
structured questions for every candidate, then follow-ups: the specific case, their own part, the result; asking
what they would do in a concrete situation counts too. Closing 4+ means the candidate left knowing the next step and
when. Selling the role for half the time is Inquiry 2. Leaving a pause after a question is a good habit.

## Focus tracks
- Get the example: asks for a specific situation, or what they would do in one, then their part and the result (Inquiry)
- Let it breathe: holds the silence after a question instead of rescuing it (Composure)
- Next step and when: closes with what happens next and by when (Closing)
