## Lens
A sales call is won on what you learn, not on what you show. Inquiry 4+ means budget, decider and timeline came out
through follow-ups, not an opening checklist, and the buyer said what the problem costs. Pushback 4+ means a
discount ask answered with a condition or a trade. Closing 4+ means the buyer committed to a dated next step; "I'll
send a proposal" with no date is Closing 2. On a big deal, a hard close backfires.

## Focus tracks
- Qualify as you go: budget, decider and timeline come out by the end of the call (Inquiry)
- Trade, never give: answers a price ask with a condition or a trade, not a cut (Pushback)
- Their dated next step: the buyer leaves owning a next step with a date (Closing)
- Sell the problem: asks what the problem costs them before showing the product (Influence)

## Drills
- Prospect: "Just send me the deck and I'll share it internally." (Closing)
- Buyer: "Your competitor is twenty percent cheaper. Can you match it?" (Pushback)
- Prospect: "We don't really have a budget for this yet." (Inquiry)
- Buyer: "Who else is using this? Give me names." (Composure)
- Prospect: "Can you show me the product first? Then we'll talk." (Influence)
- Procurement: "We need the signature by Friday, so we'll need a better number." (Pushback)
